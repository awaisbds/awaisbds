What gear ratio means
Think of it like a bicycle:

Ratio > 1 → Output spins slower but with more torque. Like climbing a hill in low gear — more force, less speed.

Ratio < 1 → Output spins faster but with less torque. Like going downhill in high gear — more speed, less force.

Ratio = 1 → Same speed, same torque. Direct drive.

The stress analogy
Imagine standing on a thin nail vs a flat shoe sole. Same weight (force), but the nail concentrates it on a tiny area — that's high stress.

Reynolds Number
—
Re = ρVD / μ

Re < 2300 → Laminar  |  2300–4000 → Transitional  |  >4000 → Turbulent
Flow regime analogy
Imagine honey vs water flowing in a pipe:

Laminar (Re < 2300) — Smooth, orderly layers. Like honey — thick and slow. Fluid particles travel in straight parallel lines.

Transitional (2300–4000) — Starts breaking up. Unstable, unpredictable.

Turbulent (Re > 4000) — Chaotic mixing. Like a fast river — swirling in all directions. Much more heat transfer but more friction loss too.
